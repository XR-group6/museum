# Voice Performance Guide

## Censer narrator

The narrator is the anthropomorphic voice of the incense burner: a mature Mandarin-speaking woman who has quietly witnessed family prayers, departure, boarding, sea crossing, and years of change.

## Direction

- Female voice only; clearly adult and feminine.
- Approximately 45-55 years old in perceived age, but not elderly.
- Warm, clear, and gently bright; slightly lowered pitch is acceptable, but avoid a heavy or masculine resonance.
- Calm, restrained, and intimate rather than dramatic.
- A very light trace of weathered texture is acceptable; avoid frailty, roughness, or excessive breathiness.
- Keep diction clear enough for subtitle synchronization and seated VR listening.

## Recording constraints

- Record the Museum and Mainland Home scripts with the same speaker identity.
- Keep room tone consistent between takes.
- Leave clean pauses at subtitle cue boundaries and before the trigger instruction.
- Record a dry master before any reverb or environmental mix is added.
