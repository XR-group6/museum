# Museum Scene Narration

## Reference script

Welcome to the museum. Please observe the incense burner in front of you. It carries a family's hopes for safety, journeys, and reunion. In Mazu belief, incense connects families with home and distant places, and witnesses prayers before leaving home, boarding a boat, and crossing the sea. You will now enter a memory inspired by historical context, moving from prayer at home toward a journey at sea. After the controller touches the incense burner, press the trigger.

Target duration: approximately 30 seconds.

## Delivery

- Calm, warm, restrained female narration.
- Mature adult voice with gentle life experience; avoid sounding elderly or theatrical.
- Keep the interaction instruction clear and slightly slower than the historical context.
- Leave a short pause before the final trigger instruction.

## Content boundary

The experience is inspired by Mazu belief and migration memories. The museum narration introduces a narrative reconstruction; it is not presented as a verbatim historical record.
