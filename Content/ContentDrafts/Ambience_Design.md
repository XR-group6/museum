# Ambience Design

These are design targets for quiet, loopable background audio. No source or licence is asserted until it is recorded in `Source_Licence_Register.md`.

## Museum

- Quiet gallery room tone.
- Very subtle ventilation or display-case electrical ambience.
- Low-level room reverb is acceptable.
- No prominent conversations, music, footsteps, or crowd noise.
- Keep the ambience below the narration and the trigger instruction.

## Mainland Home

- Quiet wooden-room or rural-home room tone.
- Soft cicadas, distant breeze, or a quiet courtyard atmosphere.
- A very subtle incense or charcoal crackle may be a secondary layer.
- Avoid cars, air conditioning, modern electronics, and obvious city noise.
- Suggest an old home and quiet waiting without competing with narration.

## Sea scene reference

Sea ambience belongs to the later boat scene and should not be used as a continuous bed in the Mainland Home scene. Candidate layers include low engine rumble, wooden hull creaks, gentle waves, distant seabirds, wind, and muffled onboard voices.
