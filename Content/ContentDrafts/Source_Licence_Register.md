## Minimum verification

- Confirm that the licence permits coursework and public demonstration.
- Record the exact source URL, creator, and access date.
- Record whether attribution is required and where it will appear.
- For AI-generated narration, record the service, generation date, prompt version, and applicable usage terms.
- Do not mark an asset as approved merely because it appears in a search result.
