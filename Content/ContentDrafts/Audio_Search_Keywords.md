# Audio Search Keywords

Use these phrases when searching libraries or field-recording sites. Check the individual licence for every result before downloading or sharing it.

## Museum

- `quiet museum gallery room tone`
- `empty exhibition hall ambience`
- `subtle gallery HVAC`
- `museum ambience no crowd`
- `quiet exhibition room reverb`

## Mainland Home

- `old wooden house room tone`
- `rural home interior ambience`
- `soft cicadas summer field recording`
- `quiet courtyard breeze`
- `subtle incense fire crackle`
- `wooden floor light footsteps`
- `packing suitcase cloth rustle`

## Sea and boat

- `small wooden boat interior ambience`
- `low ship engine rumble`
- `wooden hull creaks`
- `gentle ocean waves beside boat`
- `wind on boat deck`
- `distant seabirds over sea`
- `muffled passengers on ferry`
- `distant harbour crowd ambience`

## Search constraints

Prefer recordings that explicitly permit coursework, public demonstration, and repository sharing. Do not use a file with unclear licensing, and do not treat a search result as permission by itself.
