# Mainland Home Scene Narration

## Reference script

The memory begins in an old family home. In front of the incense burner, the family lights incense and prays to Mazu for a safe crossing, placing their hopes for reunion in the rising smoke. The furniture, luggage, and everyday objects record the moment before they leave home, board a boat, and cross the sea. Crossing the sea is not only a journey, but possibly the beginning of a new life. This scene combines historical context with artistic reconstruction. Look around.

Target duration: approximately 30 seconds.

## Delivery

- Continue the same narrator identity used in the museum scene.
- Keep the pace reflective but understandable while the player looks around.
- Emphasise `safe crossing`, `leave home`, and `new life` without becoming melodramatic.

## Content boundary

The scene combines historical context with artistic reconstruction. It should not be presented as a complete or literal historical account.
