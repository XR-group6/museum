# Narrative Continuity

## Journey across the first environments

1. **Museum** — The player observes the incense burner and receives a short introduction to its connection with family wishes, departure, and crossing the sea.
2. **Mainland Home** — The memory begins in an old home. Family members pray, prepare luggage, and face the uncertainty of leaving.
3. **Sea scene** — A later environment carries the journey onto the boat through engine rumble, waves, wind, seabirds, and distant passengers.

## Transition principle

The museum establishes the object and the player's action. The Mainland Home scene changes the context from public exhibition to personal memory. The sea scene should feel like a continuation of the departure rather than an unrelated location.

## Historical framing

The project draws on Mazu belief, family prayer, migration, and sea travel as historical and cultural context. The specific family, dialogue, timing, and spatial events are narrative reconstruction for an immersive prototype. Text and voice-over should preserve that distinction.
