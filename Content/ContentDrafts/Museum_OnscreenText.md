# Museum Scene On-Screen Text

On-screen text is separate from the spoken narration. It should provide orientation and interaction guidance without repeating every spoken sentence.

## Panel title

**Incense Burner: From Prayer to Departure**

## Information panel

The incense burner is a centre of family prayer. Incense carries hopes for safety, journeys, and reunion.

This experience is inspired by Mazu belief and memories of migration. Some scenes are narrative reconstructions rather than literal historical records.

## Interaction prompt

Observe the incense burner.

Point the controller ray at the burner. After the controller touches the burner, press the trigger to enter the memory.
